## Keywords to Avoid (for now)

**1. "How to accept crypto payments as a small business"**
CoinDesk has 5+ dedicated articles. CoinTelegraph has equivalent coverage. The organic SERP is dominated by crypto-native merchant platforms (CoinGate, BitPay, Coinbase Commerce) with high domain authority and strong commercial intent. GMGM News cannot compete here in 90 days. Revisit once the payments cluster builds internal authority.

**2. "Stablecoin taxes" / "How are stablecoins taxed"**
CoinDesk has multiple policy pieces on crypto tax. The organic SERP includes CoinLedger, TokenTax, Stripe, NerdWallet, and TurboTax adjacent sites — all with financial media authority orders of magnitude beyond a new domain. Tax queries are among the most contested in all of personal finance. Avoid until GMGM News has 6+ months of indexed content.

**3. "What is a crypto payment gateway"**
Heavily contested by fintech vendors with years of SEO investment (Technoloader, Bitpace, OxaPay, Trust Wallet, CCN). The query is broad enough to attract massive competition. CoinDesk has one sponsored post but has not fully claimed the editorial version — still, the fintech product pages are too entrenched for a new domain.

**4. "MiCA crypto regulation explained"**
CoinDesk has a dedicated "Learn" explainer page plus 8+ news articles. CoinTelegraph has comprehensive MiCA coverage going back to 2021. Decrypt, Cointelegraph's learn section, and multiple legal firms have well-indexed content. This is one of the most-covered regulatory topics in crypto media. Only viable as a hyper-narrow 2026-deadline sub-angle.

**5. "Crypto payments vs traditional payments"**
The comparison SERP is dominated by long-standing fintech infrastructure companies (Dynamic.xyz, FinchTrade, Transfi, CoinDCX, Inqud, OxaPay) with dedicated evergreen pages. The query has high search volume and high competition. Without domain authority, GMGM News will land on page 4–5. Save for the 6-month mark when the payments cluster has accrued some link equity.

## Research Notes and Caveats

- **x402 is the clearest editorial gap on the beat**: CoinDesk treats it as news (keynotes, funding), not as an explainer topic. CoinTelegraph has published nothing on x402. The window to become the canonical "what is x402" resource is open right now.
- **Agentic payments timing is tight**: Consensus Miami 2026 (May 2026) put agentic commerce at peak interest. GMGM News should publish the agentic payments explainer within 2 weeks to ride the current wave.
- **The freelancer/remote worker angle is genuinely unclaimed**: Visa's gig-worker stablecoin pilot (Nov 2025) and Belo's Latin America expansion (Apr 2026) provide strong news pegs. No editorial crypto outlet has made this audience a content priority.
- **Western Union USDPT hook has a short shelf life**: The Solana stablecoin launch news is fresh (May 2026). The evergreen remittance explainer will persist, but the news hook gives it a rankings advantage in the next 4–6 weeks.
- **Unverified — worth a manual check**: "Stablecoin vs bank transfer" — Stripe's resource pages may have stronger domain authority than the SERP check suggests. Worth testing with a manual Google search before commissioning.
