## Full Keyword List

**blockchain vs database difference explained simply**
- Competition: 🟢 LOW — Top results are TechTarget, IBM, 101blockchains, Coin Bureau, and CoinSutra. No dedicated CoinDesk, CoinTelegraph, or Decrypt page found in SERP. Results include small blogs and comparison sites.
- Intent: Informational
- Recommended format: Short-form (800–1,000 words)
- Suggested working title: "Blockchain vs. Database: What's Actually Different (And When It Matters)"
- Why this works for GMGM News: The question is evergreen, perpetually searched by beginners conflating "blockchain" with "database," and the top results don't fully nail the plain-language angle — most lean technical.

## Keywords to Avoid (for now)

**"what is blockchain technology in simple words"**
— The front page is a wall of ultra-authority: AWS, IBM, McKinsey, Wikipedia, Fidelity, Simplilearn, TechTarget, and Ledger all have dedicated pages. Over 1M search results. No gap for a new domain. Revisit at month 9+ once topical authority is established.

**"blockchain basics for beginners"**
— CoinDesk, CoinGeek, IBM, Decrypt, and E*Trade all have dedicated resources using this exact phrase. The query is too broad and the incumbents too strong. It can become an internal category page after 10+ blockchain posts exist, not a rankable article target now.

**"how does blockchain work step by step"**
— Stanford Online, IBM, and AWS sit at the top. These are domains a new crypto-news site cannot outrank on a core educational query. The result count is deep and content is comprehensive. This is a 12-month target at earliest.

**"what is a smart contract in simple terms"**
— IBM, Wikipedia, Coinbase, Chainlink, ethereum.org, Coursera, and Kaspersky dominate. The query is high-volume and well-served by authoritative content from institutions that are near-impossible to displace. Cover the concept inside a blockchain skyscraper instead.

**"what is blockchain" (head term)**
— Not a viable target. Every major financial and tech publisher — Investopedia, IBM, AWS, Wikipedia, McKinsey, and CoinDesk — has a comprehensive dedicated page. This is the canonical definition query; new domains do not rank here without years of topical authority.
