# GMGM News — Keyword Research Skill

GMGM News is a new crypto/blockchain/payments media website. Your job is to find
long-tail keywords it can realistically rank for in its first 90 days. A new domain has
zero authority, so the strategy is clear: go narrow, go specific, and win the searches
that established players ignore.

## Site Context

- **Publication**: GMGM News (new domain, no authority yet)
- **Category**: Crypto, stablecoin, blockchain, and payments news and analysis
- **Audience**: Broad tech-savvy readers — retail investors, crypto enthusiasts, Web3
  builders, finance and payments professionals
- **Content pillars**: Stablecoins & payments | Cryptocurrency | Blockchain | Regulation & policy
- **SEO goal**: Build topical authority through rankable content in the first 30–90 days

## Research Process

### Step 1: Expand the Seed Keyword

Take the input topic or seed keyword and generate 15–25 long-tail variations. Think broadly:

- **Question-based**: "what is X", "how does X work", "why is X important", "when did X start"
- **Comparison**: "X vs Y explained", "X vs Y which is better"
- **Explainer/beginner angle**: "X explained for beginners", "X simply explained"
  (these consistently have lower competition — established sites don't prioritize basics)
- **Definitional**: "what does X mean in crypto", "X definition"
- **How-to**: "how to use X", "how to buy X", "how X works step by step"
- **News-adjacent evergreen**: "what happened to X", "history of X", "timeline of X"
- **Regional/regulatory**: "X regulation in [country]", "X rules in EU", "X tax [country]"
- **Year-specific**: "X in 2025", "X 2026 guide" (these often rank quickly)
- **Use-case specific**: "X for [audience]", "X for beginners", "X for merchants"

Use WebSearch to find what people are actually searching for:
- Search the seed keyword and look at the "People Also Ask" section
- Look at "Related searches" at the bottom of results
- Note autocomplete-style variants by searching partial phrases

### Step 2: SERP Competition Assessment

For each candidate keyword, run a targeted SERP check. The goal is to estimate whether
a new site could crack the top 10 — not in 3 years, but in 60–90 days with a quality post.

**Run these searches for each keyword:**

1. Search the keyword directly. Look at the top 5 results:
   - Are they from ultra-high-authority domains (CoinDesk, Reuters, Investopedia,
     Forbes, CoinTelegraph, Wikipedia, BBC)?
   - How recent are the top results? Old content (2+ years) = opportunity.
   - Are the top results dedicated posts, or just passing mentions?

2. Check competitor coverage:
   - `site:coindesk.com "[keyword]"` — if there's a dedicated page, it's contested
   - `site:cointelegraph.com "[keyword]"` — same check
   - `site:decrypt.co "[keyword]"` — same check
   - `site:investopedia.com "[keyword]"` — often dominates informational queries

3. Note the total result count (shown as "About X results") as a rough proxy for
   competition depth. Under 100k is promising. Over 1M needs scrutiny.

**Assign a competition rating to each keyword:**

- 🟢 **LOW**: Few or no dedicated pages from major crypto media. Top results are
  Reddit threads, small blogs, or dated articles. Total results under 100k.
  A quality 800-word post could realistically land in the top 10 within 60 days.

- 🟡 **MEDIUM**: Some mid-tier crypto sites have coverage. Top 3 results are from
  strong domains, but with older content (1-2 years). Total results 100k–500k.
  A quality skyscraper could break top 20 and grow from there.

- 🔴 **HIGH**: CoinDesk, CoinTelegraph, Investopedia, or Wikipedia own the first
  page. Content is fresh and comprehensive. Total results over 500k.
  Avoid for now — GMGM News doesn't yet have the authority to compete here.

### Step 3: Search Intent Classification

Every keyword signals what the searcher wants to do. Matching intent is as important
as matching the keyword — Google demotes pages that answer the wrong question.

- **Informational** (I want to learn): "what is X", "how does X work", "X explained"
  These are the priority for a new site. Easy to write, easy to match intent.

- **Navigational** (I want to find a specific site): Skip. These are brand searches.

- **Commercial** (I want to compare options): "best X", "X vs Y", "top X wallets"
  Good for medium-term, once there's some domain trust.

- **Transactional** (I want to do something now): "buy X", "how to invest in X"
  Lowest priority for a news/media site — hard to compete with exchanges and brokers.

**For GMGM News's first 30 days, focus almost entirely on informational intent.**

### Step 4: Content Type Recommendation

Match each keyword to the right content format:

**Short-form (600–1,200 words)**
- Single, specific long-tail keyword
- Narrow, well-defined scope (one concept, one question)
- 🟢 LOW competition
- Goal: rank fast (2–8 weeks), build domain authority through volume

**Skyscraper (2,000–4,000 words)**
- A keyword cluster around a broader topic
- Structured as the definitive guide or comprehensive explainer
- 🟡 MEDIUM competition is acceptable here
- Goal: establish topical authority, attract backlinks, rank within 3–6 months

**Topical cluster logic**: A short-form post on "what is USDC" and a skyscraper on
"stablecoin guide" reinforce each other. Plan in clusters, not in isolation.

## Quality Standards

- Do not recommend a keyword without actually checking its SERP. Guessing at competition
  levels produces a useless report.
- Be specific with evidence: "Top 3 results are CoinDesk posts from 2023, no Reddit
  discussion" is useful. "High competition" alone is not.
- Be honest about uncertainty. If signals are mixed, say so and flag the keyword as
  "unverified — worth a manual check."
- Think in clusters. A single post is a gamble. Five posts covering related long-tails
  in the same topic area build authority fast.
- Prioritize keywords where GMGM News can bring a fresh angle, a clearer explanation,
  or a more current take — not just lower competition.
