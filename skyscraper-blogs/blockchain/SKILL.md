# GMGM News Skyscraper Blog Skill: Blockchain

Create comprehensive blockchain explainers that act as pillar pages for beginner
and intermediate readers. The goal is clarity, not academic density.

## Workflow

1. Validate the keyword cluster with GMGM keyword research.
2. Review top-ranking explainers and identify gaps in clarity, freshness, or examples.
3. Build a source brief using protocol documentation, reputable explainers, and current data.
4. Outline the guide around reader questions, not technical taxonomy alone.
5. Draft with diagrams described in text, examples, tradeoffs, and internal links.

## Output Standard

- Length: 2,000-4,000 words
- Structure: headline, dek, table of contents, 8-12 H2/H3 sections, conclusion
- SEO: answer featured-snippet questions directly
- Include simple examples tied to stablecoins, payments, wallets, or exchanges

## Editorial Requirements

- Define jargon before deepening.
- Compare tradeoffs honestly: decentralization, speed, cost, security, and usability.
- Avoid generic "blockchain will revolutionize everything" framing.
- Link to short-form explainers for nodes, immutability, consensus, and smart contracts.
