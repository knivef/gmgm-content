# GMGM News Skyscraper Blog Skill: Cryptocurrency

Create long-form crypto guides that give readers a durable reference without
turning into investment advice.

## Workflow

1. Validate the keyword cluster with GMGM keyword research.
2. Compare the top-ranking pages and note stale sections, missing risk framing,
   or overly promotional coverage.
3. Build a source brief from project docs, exchange documentation, filings,
   regulators, credible media, and market data.
4. Outline the guide around practical reader decisions and safety.
5. Draft with plain-English definitions, examples, tables, and current dates.

## Output Standard

- Length: 2,000-4,000 words
- Structure: headline, dek, table of contents, 8-12 H2/H3 sections, conclusion
- SEO: target one keyword cluster plus related beginner and comparison queries
- Include risk and custody sections where relevant

## Editorial Requirements

- No investment advice, price predictions, or promotional token language.
- Separate facts from interpretation.
- Explain custody, volatility, liquidity, scams, regulation, and tax uncertainty where relevant.
- Link to short-form explainers for supporting terms and concepts.
