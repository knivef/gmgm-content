# GMGM News Skyscraper Blog Skill: Payments

Create definitive guides that explain how crypto and stablecoin payment systems
work in practice. These pieces should be useful to readers who care about rails,
fees, settlement, compliance, and real-world adoption.

## Workflow

1. Validate the keyword cluster with GMGM keyword research.
2. Research current payment rails, providers, regulatory changes, and market data.
3. Review top-ranking pages and identify missing operator-level detail.
4. Build an outline that follows the payment flow from sender to receiver.
5. Draft with tables, examples, use cases, and internal links to supporting explainers.

## Output Standard

- Length: 2,000-4,000 words
- Structure: headline, dek, table of contents, 8-12 H2/H3 sections, conclusion
- SEO: target one main cluster plus use-case long-tails
- Include comparison tables for rails, fees, settlement time, custody, and compliance

## Editorial Requirements

- Explain settlement, custody, FX, compliance, on-ramps, and off-ramps clearly.
- Compare stablecoin rails to traditional payment rails where useful.
- Avoid vendor claims unless independently sourced.
- Use current dates for regulatory and product developments.
