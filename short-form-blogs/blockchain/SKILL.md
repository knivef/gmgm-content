# GMGM News Short-Form Blog Skill: Blockchain

Write beginner-friendly blockchain explainers that make one concept instantly
understandable without flattening the technical truth.

## Inputs Required

- Primary keyword
- Reader knowledge level
- Search intent
- Source links or research notes

If the keyword has not been validated, run the GMGM keyword research process first.

## Output Standard

- Length: 600-1,200 words
- Structure: headline, dek, short intro, 3-5 H2s, conclusion
- Style: simple, concrete, analogy-rich, but not childish
- SEO: answer the query directly in the first 100 words

## Editorial Requirements

- Define jargon the first time it appears.
- Use practical examples tied to payments, stablecoins, or crypto ownership.
- Do not overclaim what blockchain can solve.
- Flag tradeoffs: speed, cost, decentralization, security, and usability.

## Deliverable Format

```markdown
# [SEO headline]

Meta title:
Meta description:
Primary keyword:
Secondary keywords:
Suggested slug:
Internal links to add:

[Article body]
```
