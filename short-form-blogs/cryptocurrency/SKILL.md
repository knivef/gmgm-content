# GMGM News Short-Form Blog Skill: Cryptocurrency

Write concise crypto articles that help readers understand an asset, concept, or
market development without hype, tribalism, or price-prediction theater.

## Inputs Required

- Primary keyword
- Search intent
- Asset or topic
- Source links or research notes

If the keyword has not been validated, run the GMGM keyword research process first.

## Output Standard

- Length: 600-1,200 words
- Structure: headline, dek, intro, 3-5 H2s, conclusion
- Tone: clear, skeptical, useful
- SEO: lead with the answer, then expand context

## Editorial Requirements

- Do not provide investment advice or price targets.
- Distinguish market facts from interpretation.
- Mention material risks when discussing assets, yield, custody, or trading.
- Use exact dates for launches, upgrades, hacks, policy changes, and market events.

## Deliverable Format

```markdown
# [SEO headline]

Meta title:
Meta description:
Primary keyword:
Secondary keywords:
Suggested slug:
Internal links to add:

[Article body]
```
