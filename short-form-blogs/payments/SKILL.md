# GMGM News Short-Form Blog Skill: Payments

Write practical payment explainers that connect crypto infrastructure to real
money movement: who pays, who receives, what rails are used, and what changes.

## Inputs Required

- Primary keyword
- Search intent
- Payment use case
- Source links or research notes

If the keyword has not been validated, run the GMGM keyword research process first.

## Output Standard

- Length: 600-1,200 words
- Structure: headline, dek, intro, 3-5 H2s, conclusion
- Tone: operational, concrete, accessible
- SEO: include the use case in the title and first H2 when possible

## Editorial Requirements

- Explain the payment flow step by step.
- Compare crypto rails to cards, ACH, wires, remittances, or banking rails where relevant.
- Mention compliance, custody, FX, fees, settlement time, and consumer risk where useful.
- Avoid vendor-style promotion.

## Deliverable Format

```markdown
# [SEO headline]

Meta title:
Meta description:
Primary keyword:
Secondary keywords:
Suggested slug:
Internal links to add:

[Article body]
```
